## Video direction

- **palette system** (from `frame.md`, blue-professional remixed onto brand tokens): canvas = white; the ONE accent = KingdomApp blue `#0054ad` carries every eyebrow, numeral, CTA, progress bar, connector line, glow, checkmark; headlines near-black ink `#002a78`; body muted gray; tinted cards = 4% blue fill / 20% blue border / 10–14px radius, no shadow; all chrome pill-shaped (100px).
- **motion grammar + reveal model**: `power3` long-tail settle by default; reveals paced to voiceover cues, back ~50% of each shot carries later reveals. Module beats (checklist-card style) reveal one bullet at a time, each on its own VO phrase.
- **rhythm**: Frame 1 (portada) and Frame 16 (cierre) are the static bookend holds. Frame 4 (solución) ends on a held pivot beat. Module beats (5–15) keep a continuous build-then-brief-hold rhythm, denser than v1 (3–4 concrete bullets each, matching the reference commercial deck's level of detail — not just one summary line).
- **negative list**: no bouncy easing except earned punctuation pops; no lazy breathing; no bad back-half pan/push; no stock AI gradients; no browser chrome/real screenshots (no-capture project); no infinite/repeat/yoyo motion.
- **v2 change note**: expanded from 12 to 16 frames per explicit user feedback — v1's module beats were too thin (one sentence each); this version matches the density of the user's reference commercial PDF (checklist-card per module, 3–4 concrete capabilities) across all 9 of its module categories, plus Misiones (evangelismo, not in the PDF but a real recent module) and the "parametrizable" differentiator. Frames 1–4, 5, 6, and the visual base of 14 carry over unchanged from v1 (already at the right density); frames 7, 8, 11, 12, 13 are new; frames 9, 10, 15 are v1 frames enriched with one more concrete bullet each; 16 carries over unchanged.
